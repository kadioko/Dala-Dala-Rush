Dala Dala Rush TZ is a mobile game by Kadioko. This policy explains what data
the game handles and how it is used.

## Information We Store On Your Device

The game stores gameplay progress locally on your device, including:

- High score
- Total coins
- Unlocked vehicles
- Selected vehicle
- Language setting
- Sound and music settings
- Haptics, reduced-effects, and language settings
- Routes, missions, achievements, career upgrades, and daily/streak progress
- Optional local ghost-race, livery, and leaderboard data
- Local ad pacing and offline analytics-event logs

This local gameplay data is used only to make the game work and remember your
progress. The current analytics bridge does not upload its local event log. This
local data is not sold by us and can be removed by clearing app storage or
uninstalling the game.

## Ads

Dala Dala Rush TZ uses Google AdMob to show ads, including rewarded ads,
interstitial ads, and banner ads.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share data such as:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
- App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad/video views
- Diagnostics, such as app and SDK performance information
- Device or account identifiers, such as advertising ID and app set ID

This data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google
states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

You can learn more about Google privacy practices here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

Dala Dala Rush TZ is designed as a family-friendly game. It does not include
gambling, betting, social chat, account creation, or user-generated content.

If the app is later submitted as child-directed or included in Google Play's
Families program, ad request settings and content must be reviewed to meet all
children and families requirements.

## Purchases

The MVP version uses fake in-game coins only. It does not include real-money
gambling, betting, or real-money purchases.

Rewarded ads may offer one revive per game run or a double-coins reward after
a run. These rewards use only in-game coins and have no cash value.

## Permissions

The app may use internet and network state permissions so ads can load. It may
also use vibration for gameplay feedback. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may add the
advertising ID permission to the merged Android manifest.
