Dala Dala Rush TZ is a mobile game by Kadioko. This policy explains what data
the game handles and how it is used.

## Information We Store On Your Device

The game stores gameplay progress locally on your device, including:

- High score
- Total coins
- Unlocked vehicles
- Selected vehicle
- Language setting
- Sound and music settings
- Haptics, reduced-effects, and language settings
- Routes, missions, achievements, career upgrades, and daily/streak progress
- Neighborhood ownership/build levels and the local rent-claim date; included
  in the optional gameplay-progress backup when Cloud Backup is enabled.
  Simulated rent comes from game-controlled NPC tenants, uses fake coins, and
  is capped at 40 coins per local calendar day.
- Optional local ghost-race, livery, and leaderboard data
- Random referral invite codes, one-time confirmation codes, and local claim
  history used to prevent duplicate in-game coin rewards
- Local ad pacing and offline analytics-event logs

This local gameplay data is used only to make the game work and remember your
progress. Gameplay Insights only sends information after you choose to turn it
on in Settings. This local data is not sold by us and can be removed by
clearing app storage or uninstalling the game.

## Optional Cloud Backup And Leaderboard Pilot

Cloud Backup is optional. The game first shows an in-app consent screen and
only uploads a gameplay-progress backup after you choose to turn it on. The
Railway service stores an anonymous installation identifier, a
protected device sync credential, gameplay progress, and anonymous referral
records created through the pilot. It does not receive your email address,
contacts, precise location, advertising ID, local analytics log, or referral
codes.

Replay clips remain on the phone and are excluded from cloud backups. During
intentional phone transfer, the receiving phone saves its own random credential
before acknowledgement. The server stores hashes and temporary claim IDs;
source access is revoked only on acknowledgement. An unacknowledged code expires
after 15 minutes without revoking the source phone.

Online Leaderboards are separate from Cloud Backup and optional. Before
joining, you will be asked to choose a
short driver display name. That name and the route score you choose to submit
will be visible to other players on the friends or world leaderboard. Rankings
have no prizes and scores are labelled unverified. You can avoid this sharing
by not joining online leaderboards; the personal leaderboard remains on-device.
Players can also leave public competition from the leaderboard, which removes
their public profile, submitted scores, friend links, and active friend codes.
Players who join online competition may report a public display name using a
fixed reason; the report contains no free-text message. Players may block a
friend, which removes the friend link and prevents a new link until unblocked.
Reports and moderation snapshots are kept for up to 90 days, then scheduled for
deletion. Deleting Cloud Data also deletes reports submitted by that installation.
For the same anonymous installation, a moderation restriction remains in force
when you leave and rejoin public rankings, for the same up-to-90-day period.
Deleting Cloud Data removes the installation and that restriction record too.
Leaving rankings clears your own block preferences, not blocks other players
placed on your installation. Those remain until their owner unblocks or an
associated installation is deleted. A new installation is a different identity.

If you separately opt into hub presence, while in Kariakoo five fixed
Swahili/English phrases may briefly appear with your public driver name. No
custom text or voice is accepted. Phrases are hidden from crowd responses
after 15 seconds and cleared during service cleanup. Map presence expires
after 45 seconds. Leaving Kariakoo or turning presence off removes the active
presence row.

When the game connects to online services, the backend temporarily processes
the connection IP address in memory to enforce request limits and reduce abuse.
The game backend does not write this address to the game database or use it to
infer location. The hosting/network provider may process connection metadata
under its own privacy practices.

## Optional Gameplay Insights

Gameplay Insights have their own in-app opt-in separate from Cloud Backup and
Online Leaderboards. After you opt in, the game sends a small allowlist of
sanitized gameplay events associated with an anonymous installation identifier.
Events include tutorial starts, steps, attempts and completion, rewarded-ad
requests, loads, load failures and earned rewards, route and run outcomes (including score,
distance, elapsed time, and end reason), route-moment starts/completions/timeouts,
fuel failures, vehicle unlock type/price/run count, leaderboard request
success/failure, and day-1/day-7 return markers. Reports use aggregated counts
and do not include player-entered names.
Each uploaded event includes a random event identifier to prevent counting
network retries twice; it is not an advertising identifier.
Completed runs also carry a random logical run ID so revives do not count as
another completed run. Explicit debug frame captures stay local and are not
uploaded to Railway.
The service rejects names, friend codes, tokens, advertising IDs, contact
details, and free-form nested data. You can turn this option off in Settings at
any time; this stops future uploads. Gameplay Insights events and leaderboard
reports are scheduled for deletion after 90 days. Cleanup is periodic, so
service interruptions may delay removal until service resumes.

You can turn Cloud Backup off at any time. The in-game **Delete Online Data**
control permanently deletes the anonymous installation identity, election
votes, cloud backup, online scores, referral records, and temporary transfer
codes. It does not delete the game progress stored on your phone. We will keep
this policy and the Google Play Data Safety form updated if this service's data
handling changes.

The referral feature does not read or upload your contacts, phone number, name,
account, or precise location. Sharing opens the Android share sheet, and you
choose the app and recipient yourself.

## Ads

Dala Dala Rush TZ uses Google AdMob to show ads, including rewarded ads,
interstitial ads, and banner ads.

Google UMP's current SDK decision gates native ad requests; required forms are
shown and required Ad Privacy Options appear in Settings. Advertising choices
are separate from the three optional Railway services.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share data such as:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
- App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad/video views
- Diagnostics, such as app and SDK performance information
- Device or account identifiers, such as advertising ID and app set ID

This data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google
states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

You can learn more about Google privacy practices here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

Dala Dala Rush TZ is designed as a family-friendly game. It does not include
gambling, betting or social chat. No email/password login is required. Optional
online rankings contain player-entered public display names with reporting and
moderation controls.

If the app is later submitted as child-directed or included in Google Play's
Families program, ad request settings and content must be reviewed to meet all
children and families requirements.

## Purchases

The MVP version uses fake in-game coins only. It does not include real-money
gambling, betting, or real-money purchases.

Rewarded ads may offer one revive per game run or a double-coins reward after
a run. These rewards use only in-game coins and have no cash value.

## Permissions

The app may use internet and network state permissions so ads can load. It may
also use vibration for gameplay feedback. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may add the
advertising ID permission to the merged Android manifest.
