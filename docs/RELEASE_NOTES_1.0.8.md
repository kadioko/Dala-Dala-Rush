# Dala Dala Rush TZ 1.0.8

## English

- Every route now drives like a different job, with its own passenger rhythm,
  stop spacing, fuel pressure, weather character, rush-hour chance, and
  signature moment.
- Added Driver Reputation, Route Mastery, Driving Flow, better run highlights,
  and a guided first-session journey.
- Improved results feedback and vehicle unlock explanations in Swahili and
  English.
- Added safer live balance controls and improved offline analytics reliability.

## Kiswahili

- Kila route sasa ina kazi yake: mpangilio wa abiria, vituo, mafuta, hali ya
  hewa, rush hour na tukio maalum.
- Kuna Kiwango cha Huduma, Route Mastery, Driving Flow, mambo bora ya safari
  na mafunzo mafupi ya mwanzo.
- Matokeo na maelezo ya dala dala mpya yameboreshwa kwa Kiswahili na English.
- Tumeongeza njia salama za kubalance mchezo na kuboresha analytics za offline.

## Tester Focus
