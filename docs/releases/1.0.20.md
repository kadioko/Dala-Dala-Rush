# Dala Dala Rush TZ 1.0.20

## Release name

Mtaa Wetu: Neighborhood Life

## English

Explore all six districts in Mtaa Wetu, take on local activities, collect
district keepsakes, and personalize your properties with buildable decorations.
Meet familiar characters, manage your property portfolio, and use the optional
Mtaa Phone for quick neighborhood services. The Kariakoo lounge uses preset
messages only; no open text or voice chat.

## Kiswahili

Tembelea mitaa sita ya Mtaa Wetu, fanya shughuli za kila eneo, kusanya kumbukumbu
za wilaya na pamba viwanja vyako. Kutana na wahusika unaowafahamu, simamia
mali zako, na utumie Simu ya Mtaa kwa huduma za haraka. Ukumbi wa Kariakoo una
ujumbe maalum pekee; hakuna gumzo la maandishi huru au sauti.
