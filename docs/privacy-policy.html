Dala Dala Rush TZ Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Dala Dala Rush TZ is a mobile game by Kadioko. This policy explains what data the game handles and how it is used.

Information Stored On Your Device

The game stores gameplay progress locally on your device, including scores, coins, unlocked content, selected vehicle and route, settings, missions, achievements, career progress, daily rewards, liveries, ghost races, local leaderboard entries, random referral invite and confirmation codes, local referral claim history, ad pacing, and an offline analytics-event log.

This local gameplay data is used only to make the game work and remember your progress. Gameplay Insights only sends information after you choose to turn it on in Settings. This data is not sold by us and can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the game.

The referral feature does not read or upload your contacts, phone number, name, account, or precise location. Sharing opens the Android share sheet, and you choose the app and recipient yourself.

Optional Cloud Backup And Leaderboard Pilot

Cloud Backup is optional. The game first shows an in-app consent screen and only uploads a gameplay-progress backup after you choose to turn it on.

The Railway service stores an anonymous installation identifier, a protected device sync credential, gameplay progress, and anonymous referral records created through the pilot. It does not receive your email address, contacts, precise location, advertising ID, local analytics log, or referral codes.

Online Leaderboards are separate from Cloud Backup and optional. Before joining, you will choose a short driver display name. That name and submitted route scores will be visible to other players on friends or world leaderboards. Rankings have no prizes and scores are labelled unverified. You can avoid this sharing by not joining; your personal leaderboard remains on-device. You can also leave online competition to remove your public profile, submitted scores, friend links, and active friend codes.

Players who join online competition can report a public display name using a fixed reason, or block a friend. Reports are reviewed for abuse; they do not include a free-text message. Reports and moderation snapshots are retained for up to 90 days and then scheduled for deletion. Blocking removes the friend link and prevents future friend-code linking between those players.

When the game connects to online services, the backend temporarily processes the connection IP address in memory to enforce request limits and reduce abuse. It stores a keyed, pseudonymous HMAC of the address with the endpoint and time window in PostgreSQL so limits are shared across service restarts and replicas. These counters expire at the end of the applicable limit window and are removed during cleanup. The raw address is not written to the game database or used to infer location. The hosting/network provider may process connection metadata under its own privacy practices.

You can turn Cloud Backup off at any time. The in-game Delete Cloud Data control permanently deletes the anonymous installation's cloud backup, online scores, referral records, and temporary transfer codes. It does not delete progress stored on your phone. We will keep this policy and the Google Play Data Safety form updated if this service's data handling changes.

Optional Gameplay Insights

Gameplay Insights has a separate in-app opt-in. After you opt in, the game sends a small allowlist of sanitized gameplay events associated with an anonymous installation identifier. Events include tutorial completion; route and run outcomes, including score, distance, run duration, and end reason; route-moment starts, completions, and timeouts; fuel failures; vehicle unlock type, coin price, and run count; leaderboard request success or failure; and day-1/day-7 return markers. Reports use aggregated counts and do not include player-entered names.

The service rejects names, friend codes, tokens, advertising IDs, contact details, and free-form nested data. Turning Gameplay Insights off stops future uploads.

Gameplay Insights events and leaderboard reports are scheduled for deletion after 90 days. Cleanup runs periodically, so service interruptions may delay removal until service resumes. Deleting Cloud Data also removes events and reports submitted by that installation.

Ads

Dala Dala Rush TZ uses Google AdMob to show ads, including rewarded ads, interstitial ads, and banner ads.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share data such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. This data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

You can learn more about Google privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Dala Dala Rush TZ is designed as a family-friendly game. It does not include gambling, betting, social chat, account creation, or user-generated content.

If the app is later submitted as child-directed or included in Google Play's Families program, ad request settings and content must be reviewed to meet all children and families requirements.

Purchases

The MVP version uses fake in-game coins only. It does not include real-money gambling, betting, or real-money purchases.

Permissions

The app may use internet and network state permissions so ads can load. It may also use vibration for gameplay feedback. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may add the advertising ID permission to the Android app.
